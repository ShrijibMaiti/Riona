# Surface brief: Riona public site, sign-in, app and client portal

Scope and mode: the public site (Persuade) plus sign-in, the internal app and the client portal (Operate), restyled as one world.
Audience: owners, HR leads and managers at Indian IT services firms (20–200 staff); faculty evaluators while it is an academic project.
Job: show that one timesheet entry becomes payroll cost, an invoice line and project margin, then get the visitor signed in.
Proof: real Kestrel Digital demo figures only. No customer logos, counts, or roadmap features presented as built.
Constraints: the user pinned the world, the "Stratum" infrastructure sample from the impeccable gallery (gpt-6-astra, 21-infra-platform, sample 0545e2673bc2aacf). Its design philosophy and palette, Geist plus IBM Plex Mono, a drawn technical figure in place of the portal photo, every earlier critique fix folded in.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Riona is a measured instrument for a services firm's ledger. One entry is drawn and annotated like a technical figure, and every number is shown with where it came from. It refuses the glowing dark "workplace ecosystem" hero and the icon-tile hub grid.

OWN-WORLD:
- Palette:
  - paper #F7F7F2;
  - olive-black ink #252823;
  - muted #676A61;
  - hairlines #DFE1D7;
  - one signal orange #EF582E for decoration, and #D4421F wherever it carries white text;
  - healthy green #64804B;
  - one dark olive field #272D22.
- Components:
  - the page sits inside a frame of vertical hairlines with "+" crosshairs where the section rules cross it;
  - near-square buttons (4 px radius) with ↗ or →;
  - Geist 500–550 headings with tight tracking;
  - IBM Plex Mono only for data: IDs, units, figure captions and annotations;
  - status shown as a dot plus text.

STORY: the visitor sees one real entry (Aarav Shah, Fleet Ops Portal) split into three figures, changes the hours and watches the figures recompute, scrubs a salary history, sees what each role gets, reads honest answers, and signs in.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Left: a 76 px Geist headline over a two-line lead, a primary orange "Sign in" button and a secondary "See one entry become three figures ↓", with a mono note underneath.
- Right: FIG. 01, an isometric line drawing of the timesheet entry slab sitting over three ledger slabs, each with a leader line to a mono annotation carrying its real figure.
- Below the fold line: the strip of statutory items Riona computes.

FORM: Stratum's framed blueprint grammar, translated, with these deviations:
- No kicker or eyebrow above any heading (a craft-floor ban), so section numbers become FIG. captions on figures.
- Status red is kept distinct from the brand orange.
- seed: none (pinned). The user's words: "apply this websites https://impeccable.style/gallery/?model=gpt-6-astra&niche=21-infra-platform&sample=0545e2673bc2aacf#21-infra-platform design philosophy & colour scheme"

Signature interaction: the entry editor. Changing hours or flipping "billable" recomputes payroll cost, invoice line and margin live, with the arithmetic shown in mono.

Motion grammar: the figure's leader lines draw once, and numbers settle with an exponential ease-out. Nothing loops. Reduced motion disables all of it.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
